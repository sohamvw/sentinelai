# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023)
Source: The Gazette of India, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
Authority: Parliament of India. Effective date: notified in tranches from 2024-2025.
This file contains the canonical rules that SentinelAI's policy checker enforces
when the sector is "indian_dpdp" or "indian_general".

## Section 4 — Grounds for processing personal data
A Data Fiduciary may process personal data only for a lawful purpose for which
the Data Principal has given consent, or for certain legitimate uses specified
in the Act. Processing without a lawful basis is prohibited.

## Section 6 — Notice
The Data Fiduciary must issue a notice to the Data Principal describing the
personal data and the purpose of processing before such processing is undertaken.

## Section 7 — Accuracy and completeness
Personal data processed must be accurate, complete, and not misleading.

## Section 8 — Storage limitation and retention
Personal data shall not be retained beyond the period necessary to satisfy the
purpose for which it was collected.

## Section 8(4) — Erasure on withdrawal of consent
Upon withdrawal of consent, the Data Fiduciary must erase personal data unless
retention is necessary for legal purposes.

## Section 9 — Security safeguards (CRITICAL)
The Data Fiduciary shall protect personal data in its possession by taking
reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach. Where a
breach is likely to cause harm, the Data Fiduciary must inform the Data
Protection Board and affected Data Principals.

## Section 10 — Accountability and grievance redressal
Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) and
an independent data auditor, and publish business contact information.

## Section 11 — Right of Data Principal to access
A Data Principal has the right to obtain a summary of personal data and the
identities of all Data Fiduciaries with whom such data has been shared.

## Section 12 — Right to correction and erasure
The Data Principal has the right to correct and erase their personal data.

## Section 17 — Exemptions
Exemptions apply for national security, public order, and certain research
purposes, but do not override Section 9 security safeguards.

## Section 33 — Penalties
Breach of Section 9 can attract penalties up to ₹250 crore.

## Section 44 — Cross-border transfer
The Central Government may restrict the transfer of personal data for
processing to a country or territory outside India through a negative-list
approach.
